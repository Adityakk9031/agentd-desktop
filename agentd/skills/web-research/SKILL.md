---
name: web-research
description: Research a question on the web and answer with sources.
---

1. Break the question into two to four searches and run them with web_search.
2. Open the most relevant results with fetch_url (use the browser only for pages that need clicking or a login).
3. Prefer recent, primary sources. Note the date of anything time-sensitive, like prices or news.
4. Check important facts in two independent sources. Say plainly when sources disagree or you could not confirm something.
5. Answer briefly first, then the details, and list the addresses you used.

Never fill gaps from memory as if they were findings.
